Your SUNY Poly-provisioned Claude account is a SUNY Poly IT system, the
same as your student email or your Brightspace login. That means it's
governed by the same institutional policy that governs all of them.

**Read the full policy here before you continue:**
[sunypoly.edu/its/policies/appropriate-use-of-information-technology<span class="visually-hidden">
(external
site)</span>](https://sunypoly.edu/its/policies/appropriate-use-of-information-technology.html)

## Why this page exists

This page isn't a substitute for the policy at that link. It's short on
purpose: the policy is the authority, not a summary of it. What follows
is orientation, not the terms — the terms are at the link.

## What it covers, in outline

  - **Who it applies to.** Every user of a SUNY Poly IT system —
    students, faculty, staff — including use from personal equipment
    when it's reaching a SUNY Poly system.
  - **The general principle.** Use has to relate to your academic role
    and responsibilities, and has to comply with the law and with SUNY
    Poly's other policies. Personal use is allowed only if it stays
    incidental.
  - **What's specifically prohibited.** Among the categories:
    interfering with other users' access; harassing or threatening use;
    attempting to defeat system security or gain unauthorized access;
    impersonation or disguising your identity; distributing malware;
    illegal use; violating software licensing terms; and using the
    systems for private commercial gain.
  - **Monitoring.** SUNY Poly can examine account activity without
    consent under specific conditions the policy lists — a security or
    system-integrity need, a legal requirement, a suspected policy
    violation, and a few others.
  - **Enforcement.** Violations go through the ordinary student,
    faculty, or staff disciplinary process, and unlawful use can carry
    civil or criminal liability on top of that.

## Why this matters for this course

This is the account-level layer underneath everything in the Claude
Account & Acceptable Use Policy. That document covers what's specific to
your COM 216 / FYS 111 Claude account — token budget, Workbench sharing,
the observation-checkpoint process. This policy covers what's true of
every SUNY Poly IT account, this one included.
