## What a recording is good for

Use it to recover something specific: a link that went by too fast, an
instruction about the workbench you want to get exactly right, a thread of
discussion you want to quote in an observation. Scrubbing to the part you
need is almost always a better use of your time than replaying the session
end to end.

## What it does not replace

Being in the room. Much of what happens in this class is discussion, and
discussion is not a thing you can catch up on by watching it happen to
other people. If you missed this session, watch the recording and then come
to the next one with a question from it — that is how the gap actually
closes.
