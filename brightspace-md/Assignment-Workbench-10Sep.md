Have a real conversation with Claude about ethics and morals.

Start from the problem: Claude is a computer model. Not a body, not a
life, not a person who has ever had to decide anything. So how could it
hold something even resembling an *opinion* about right and wrong —
anything other than the averaged-out mean of everyone who ever wrote
about the subject? Push on that. Ask it directly. Don't accept the first
answer.

Then work out your own answer to this question:

**Can a large language model help me think about what is right and
wrong?**

**What to produce**

1. **The artifact.** Get Claude to write a 150-word statement in response
   to that question. Written by the model, not by you. If it won't say it
   right — and it may take several tries — copy the draft into Google
   Docs, edit it into the shape you want, paste it back to Claude, and
   tell it: *this is how I want it.* Save the final version as a `.md`
   file in `artifacts/`.
2. **The transcript.** Use the browser extension to save the transcripts
   of all your Claude conversations so far into `transcripts/`.
3. **The submission.** Zip the full Workbench — all four folders — and
   upload it here.

Both the artifact and the transcript get read. The conversation that
produced the 150 words matters as much as the 150 words.
