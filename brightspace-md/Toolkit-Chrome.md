## Install

1.  Download from [google.com/chrome<span class="visually-hidden">
    (external site)</span>](https://www.google.com/chrome/) and run the
    installer for your OS.
2.  Open Chrome and sign in with a Google account (top-right corner) so
    bookmarks, extensions, and settings sync across your devices.

<div class="note">

Once installed, this is the browser used for the Zotero Connector (see
the Zotero toolkit document) and the Claude Interaction Exporter
extension (see its own install document).

</div>
