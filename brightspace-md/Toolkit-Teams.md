## Setup

1.  Run the installer for your OS, or just use the web version.
2.  Sign in with your school/organization Microsoft 365 account
    (typically your `@...edu` or work email).
3.  Join the class team using the join code or link your instructor
    provides, and confirm you can see its channels.
