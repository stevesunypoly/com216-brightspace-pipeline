Stream has a transcript. Open the transcript panel beside the video to read along, search the session for a term you half-remember, or jump straight to the moment something was discussed. You can also change the playback speed. Both are worth knowing about before you sit down to rewatch an hour of class.

What a recording is good for

Use it to recover something specific: a link that went by too fast, an instruction about the workbench you want to get exactly right, a thread of discussion you want to quote in an observation. Scrubbing to the part you need is almost always a better use of your time than replaying the session end to end.

What it does not replace

Being in the room. Much of what happens in this class is discussion, and discussion is not a thing you can catch up on by watching it happen to other people. If you missed this session, watch the recording and then come to the next one with a question from it — that is how the gap actually closes.
