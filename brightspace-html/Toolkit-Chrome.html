Chrome Browser

The browser used throughout the toolkit — for the Zotero Connector and the Claude Interaction Exporter extension.

Install

  1. Download from google.com/chrome (external site) and run the installer for your OS.
  2. Open Chrome and sign in with a Google account (top-right corner) so bookmarks, extensions, and settings sync across your devices.
Once installed, this is the browser used for the Zotero Connector (see the Zotero toolkit document) and the Claude Interaction Exporter extension (see its own install document).
