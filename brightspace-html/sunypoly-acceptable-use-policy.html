SUNY Poly Appropriate Use of Information Technology

Your SUNY Poly-provisioned Claude account is a SUNY Poly IT system, the same as your student email or your Brightspace login. That means it's governed by the same institutional policy that governs all of them.

Read the full policy here before you continue: sunypoly.edu/its/policies/appropriate-use-of-information-technology (external site)

Why this page exists

This page isn't a substitute for the policy at that link. It's short on purpose: the policy is the authority, not a summary of it. What follows is orientation, not the terms — the terms are at the link.

What it covers, in outline

  • Who it applies to. Every user of a SUNY Poly IT system — students, faculty, staff — including use from personal equipment when it's reaching a SUNY Poly system.
  • The general principle. Use has to relate to your academic role and responsibilities, and has to comply with the law and with SUNY Poly's other policies. Personal use is allowed only if it stays incidental.
  • What's specifically prohibited. Among the categories: interfering with other users' access; harassing or threatening use; attempting to defeat system security or gain unauthorized access; impersonation or disguising your identity; distributing malware; illegal use; violating software licensing terms; and using the systems for private commercial gain.
  • Monitoring. SUNY Poly can examine account activity without consent under specific conditions the policy lists — a security or system-integrity need, a legal requirement, a suspected policy violation, and a few others.
  • Enforcement. Violations go through the ordinary student, faculty, or staff disciplinary process, and unlawful use can carry civil or criminal liability on top of that.
