The Prominent Role of AI in This Course

Start here: AI is not a tool, and it is not a person. It's something we don't really have a word for yet — a genuinely exotic kind of mind, built by us, out of our own written record, that can meet us in language and generate. Not human. Not pretending to be human. Something new enough that treating it like either a hammer or a colleague will mislead you.

That's the posture this course asks you to take. Every augmentation before this one frightened people when it arrived — writing, the alphabet, the printing press, the calculator — and every time, the fear turned out to be about losing ourselves, not about the tool itself. This one is different in kind, not just degree: it doesn't just carry human thought the way a page or a wire does, it originates content and makes decisions. That crossing matters, and it's part of why the course asks you to locate your own judgment rather than borrow someone else's.

Every material in this course, this document included, is generated by AI, through a process I work at deliberately. These materials are my own attempt to read, think, and write with AI at a professional level, and as the semester goes on I'll show you how they were built and how they keep getting built. I don't hide the AI use. I guide it, but only up to a point, and I don't read every word it generates.

So think of AI the way you'd think about an unfamiliar species that has arrived and isn't leaving — not a threat to defend against, not a magic assistant to surrender to, but something exotic enough to warrant real attention before you decide how to live alongside it. This course is that attention, sustained over a semester. You'll build the habit of putting the model in the loop deliberately — not to think for you, but to think with — and you'll do it by working out, case by case, where you actually stand.
